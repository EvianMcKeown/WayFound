WAYFOUND HELP VIDEO: THE SCRIPT IN PLAIN TEXT
=============================================

The recording plays scripts/video/storyboard.mjs. This file is the same script in plain words, with the
captions exactly as they appear on screen. Change the wording or the order here, then tell me, or edit the
matching lines in storyboard.mjs yourself (each caption below is one caption(...) line there).

It is a hybrid of showcase and help video: every gesture achieves something (opens the search, plans a trip,
reveals the steps) and is never shown for its own sake, and the captions lead with the benefit. No voiceover
or music: the captions carry it. Roughly 65 seconds. A thin green progress bar grows
along the bottom edge for the whole video (the video player's own controls are meant to be hidden).

The video plays signed in as a demo user ("Alex"), so "Save this route" really changes to "Saved".


SCENE 1. INTRO                                                  0:00 - 0:04
  Picture   Brand card: the WayFound mark, and the name and line under it flip down into place, word by
            word. The app is already loaded behind it; the card dissolves into the app.
  On card   "Every bus and train. One map."
  Captions  none


SCENE 2. DESKTOP: FROM TWO PLACES TO A SAVED TRIP
  Picture   The app in a laptop-sized window, signed in as the demo user. The trip is Gugulethu to the V&A
            Waterfront, which uses all three operators: Golden Arrow, Metrorail and MyCiTi (with walks).

  CAPTION  "Start anywhere in Cape Town"
        - The camera zooms on the search panel. The pointer appears at the From field and clicks it. Types
          "Gugulethu" (quickly); the first suggestion is chosen.

  CAPTION  "Go anywhere else"
        - Pointer moves to the To field. Types "V&A Waterfront"; the first suggestion is chosen.
        - Pointer clicks Find route, then disappears. The camera pulls back as the route draws on the map.

  CAPTION  "Three operators, one journey"
        - Hold on the whole screen: route on the map, trip in the panel.

  CAPTION  "Every operator in its own colour"
        - The camera zooms once, on the list of legs and the Save button together, and does not move again.
          A small tag appears beside each leg in turn: On foot, Golden Arrow, Metrorail, MyCiTi.

  CAPTION  "Taking it again? Save it for next time"
        - Same view. The pointer clicks Save this route; the button changes to "Saved" with a small pop of the
          bookmark. The pointer disappears and the camera pulls back out.


TRANSITION. A frozen frame of the laptop scene dissolves into the phone scene, with the camera easing in.


SCENE 3. PHONE: PLAN, READ, CHANGE
  Picture   A phone frame in the middle of the screen. Captions sit beside the phone so the bottom sheet
            stays in view. A finger marker shows each swipe and tap; there is no mouse pointer.

  CAPTION  "Your whole city, in your pocket"
        - Hold on the empty planner: map full screen, "Where to?" bar at the bottom.

  CAPTION  "Swipe up to start planning"
        - Finger swipes up from the "Where to?" bar; the search sheet opens.

  CAPTION  "Search any place by name"
        - Finger taps From; types "Gugulethu"; taps the first suggestion.
        - Finger taps To; types "V&A Waterfront"; taps the first suggestion.
        - Finger taps Find route; the sheet folds to a summary and the route draws on the map.

  CAPTION  "Swipe up for step-by-step directions"
        - A moment on the trip, then the finger swipes up; the sheet grows to show every step.

  CAPTION  "Swipe down for the full map"
        - Finger swipes down; the sheet shrinks and the whole map is back in view.

  CAPTION  "Less walking? Re-plan instantly"
        - Finger taps the pencil; the "Change journey" form opens over the trip.
        - Finger taps Options, then "Minimise walking", then Find route; the route redraws with less walking.


SCENE 4. OUTRO
  Picture   The closing card dissolves in over the phone (its line flips down word by word), holds, then the
            whole video fades out.
  On card   "Your next trip, planned in seconds."
  Captions  none


NOTES FOR EDITING
  - Keep each caption to about six words and give it at least 1.5 seconds.
  - The same captions are saved as subtitles (help-video.vtt), so the wording here is what viewers who
    cannot hear or see the picture well will read.
  - Scene lengths are set by the "wait" lines in storyboard.mjs, not by this file.
