WAYFOUND HELP VIDEO: THE SCRIPT IN PLAIN TEXT
=============================================

The recording plays scripts/video/storyboard.mjs. This file is the same script in plain words, with the
captions exactly as they appear on screen. Change the wording or the order here, then tell me, or edit the
matching lines in storyboard.mjs yourself (each caption below is one caption(...) line there).
`node scripts/video/record.mjs --check` says whether the two still agree (every render checks too).

It is a hybrid of showcase and help video: every gesture achieves something (opens the search, plans a trip,
reveals the steps) and is never shown for its own sake, and the captions lead with the benefit. No voiceover
or music: the captions carry it. The story is one trip, followed from the laptop to the phone: planned,
compared, adjusted and saved on the laptop, then opened from Saved routes on the phone. A thin green progress
bar grows along the bottom edge for the whole video (the video player's own controls are meant to be hidden).

The video plays signed in as a demo user ("Alex"). The video server remembers the route saved on the laptop, so
"Save this route" really changes to "Saved" and the phone really finds it under Saved routes.

Timings below are from the last render (about 82 seconds in all); every render prints the real ones at the end.

SCENE 2. DESKTOP: ONE TRIP, PLANNED, ADJUSTED AND SAVED          0:03 - 0:50
  Picture   The app in a laptop-sized window, signed in as the demo user. The trip is Gugulethu to the V&A

  CAPTION  "Start anywhere in Cape Town"

  CAPTION  "…and end anywhere"
        - Pointer moves to the To field. Types "V&A Waterfront"; the first suggestion is chosen.
        - Pointer clicks Find route, then disappears. The camera pulls back as the route draws on the map.

  CAPTION  "Three operators, one journey"
        - Hold on the whole screen: route on the map, trip in the panel. (This is the poster still.)

  CAPTION  "Every operator in its own colour"

  CAPTION  "Not the best fit? Compare routes"

          other routes appear as cards (each with its duration, transfers and walking, and a label such as

          change to it.

  CAPTION  "Rather skip a line? Avoid it"
        - The pointer clicks Avoid beside a MyCiTi ride. The pointer disappears and the trip is planned again
          without that line.

  CAPTION  "Re-planned without it. Undo anytime"
        - The panel scrolls back to the top: the notice "Avoiding MyCiTi ..." with its Undo, and the
          "Avoiding 1" tag on the headline.

  CAPTION  "Taking it again? Save it"
        - The panel scrolls to the bottom and the camera zooms on Save this route. The pointer clicks it; the
          button changes to "Saved" with a small pop of the bookmark. The camera pulls back out.


TRANSITION. A frozen frame of the laptop scene dissolves into the phone scene, with the camera easing in.

NOTES FOR EDITING
  - Keep each caption to six words or fewer and give it at least 1.5 seconds; the render warns otherwise.
  - The same captions are saved as subtitles (help-video.vtt), so the wording here is what viewers who
    cannot hear or see the picture well will read.
  - Scene lengths are set by the "wait" lines in storyboard.mjs, not by this file.
  - The Undo and Saved-routes beats show real app features; if the app's wording changes ("Avoiding",
    "Saved routes", "Plan"), the storyboard's selectors and these captions need the same change.
